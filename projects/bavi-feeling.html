Bavi Feeling

Bavi Feeling is where I did my mandatory university practice, and my first close look at how an online brand works from the inside. This page covers what I learned there, from creating and editing product photoshoots to the process behind a Shopify store, and how it all shaped the marketing work I do today for beoopsie.

Start with the photoshoots ↓ Dates

Product Photoshoots & Editing

During the practice I learned how to create product photoshoots: arranging the products, working with light and angles, and shooting the images an online store actually needs. I then edited the photos myself, turning the raw shots into clean, consistent visuals ready for the website. It is the same process I now use for every beoopsie product launch.

Behind a Shopify Store

I also got to see the process behind a Shopify store: how products go from a photoshoot to a finished product page, and what it takes to keep an online shop running day to day. Seeing a small brand work end to end gave me the foundation for the marketing work I now do for beoopsie, including building and managing its Shopify store.
