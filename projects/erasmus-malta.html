Erasmus+ Malta

In October 2026 I travelled to Malta for an Erasmus+ program built around POWER, a project on the Prevention Of Weaponization and Enhancing Resilience against security-related disinformation on clean energy. This page covers what the Clean Energy Café taught me about clean energy technologies and the disinformation that targets the energy transition, and how we turned that knowledge into a hands-on campaign.

Start with the Clean Energy Café ↓ 25/10/2026 – 30/10/2026

Inside the Clean Energy Café

The program followed POWER's Clean Energy Café, a face-to-face format also held in Romania and Spain. We started with the foundations of clean energy technologies, then looked at the disinformation narratives aimed at the energy transition and learned how to identify and analyse them. It showed me where clean energy and security meet, and why false claims about energy are more than a communication problem.

From Science to Campaign

The second part of the program moved from analysis to practice. We developed our science communication skills, learning how to explain complex topics clearly, and then applied everything in a hands-on integrated campaign. As a marketer, this was the part closest to my own work: a reminder that a clear message matters most when the facts themselves are being contested.
