Work and Travel 2024

In the summer of 2024 I took part in a Work and Travel program in Minnesota, where I held two jobs at Mall of America in Bloomington, the largest mall in the United States. This page covers both roles, as a ride operator at Nickelodeon Universe and as an Entertainment Team Member at Crayola Experience, and what three months alongside an international team taught me about working with people.

Start with Mall of America ↓ 06/2024 – 10/2024

Nickelodeon Universe & Crayola Experience

At Nickelodeon Universe, the indoor theme park inside Mall of America, I worked as a ride operator, running rides and looking after the guests who lined up for them all day. My second role was at Crayola Experience, as an Entertainment Team Member, in direct contact with families and children from across the country. Both jobs taught me to stay calm, clear and friendly while a constant flow of guests moved through.

Working in an International Team

Both jobs brought me into an international team of colleagues from many countries, and for three months we worked long, demanding and genuinely fun hours together. The experience strengthened my communication skills and helped me build real relationships in a multicultural environment, which is something I still rely on today when I work with partners from different markets.
